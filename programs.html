Our sessions

Good conversations about everyday health

Each session is a chance to learn something practical, share what has worked for you, or simply listen. They focus on general wellbeing, rather than diagnosis or treatment.

01 / FOOD

What’s for dinner?

Bring a recipe you like, or hear what other people make at home.

Food and cooking Practical ideas

02 / MOVE

A bit more movement

Share ideas for moving a little more during the day. Go at your own pace.

Movement Take it at your pace

03 / REST

Getting enough rest

Talk about bedtime routines and what helps you unwind at the end of the day.

Rest and sleep Open conversation

04 / STRESS

When things feel stressful

Try a breathing exercise or talk about what helps on a difficult day. Sharing is up to you.

Stress No pressure to share

05 / FIRST AID

First aid: a first look

Talk about when to call for help and how to keep someone safe while you wait.

First aid Not a certification course

06 / WELLBEING

A chance to check in

Take a moment to check in with yourself and think about who you can talk to.

Wellbeing Open to everyone

What to expect

A little more about each session

Sessions are relaxed and practical, with time for questions and discussion.

Session topics and format
Topic What we'll cover Format Length
What’s for dinner? Simple meals and food planning Group discussion 60 minutes
A bit more movement Everyday ways to stay active Talk and light activity 45 minutes
Getting enough rest Sleep routines and winding down Group discussion 45 minutes

First aid: a first look Getting help and staying safe Guided discussion 60 minutes

Health terms

What these words mean

Wellbeing
How you feel physically, mentally and in everyday life.
Health literacy
Finding health information and understanding it.
Prevention
Things that may lower the chance of some health problems.
First aid
Basic help for someone who is hurt or suddenly unwell while waiting for medical help.
