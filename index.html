About Harbor Health

A friendly place to start

Health advice can feel like a lot to take in. We keep the conversation practical and make room for questions, shared experiences and different points of view.

Our sessions cover everyday topics like food, rest and movement. They don't replace medical care. For personal health concerns, speak with a qualified health professional.

A few ideas

Start with what feels manageable.

  1. Take a short walk if that works for you.
  2. Stand up and stretch after sitting for a while.
  3. Try going to bed around the same time for a few nights.
  4. Ask a health professional if you have a health concern.

Taking part

Join in your own way.

  • Ask questions whenever they come up.
  • Share your thoughts or just listen.
  • Take part at a pace that works for you.
  • Keep personal details to yourself.

Health words

A few useful words

Wellbeing
How you’re doing physically, mentally and in your day-to-day life.
Prevention
Things people can do that may lower the chance of some health problems.
Health literacy
Being able to find and make sense of health information.
